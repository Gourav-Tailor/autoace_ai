How AudoAck works

A simple workflow from audio capture to analysis results.

🎙️
1. Record

Record short audio segments using the app's microphone-based recording feature.

☁️
2. Upload

Audio is sent to the AudoAck backend when it is submitted for analysis.

📊
3. Analyze

The analysis pipeline produces audio signals and confidence information.

📱
4. Review

Review the latest available analysis associated with your devices.

Analysis signals

AudoAck currently presents analysis signals derived from submitted recordings.

Emotional tone
Detected tone categories and confidence values.
Intensity
Low, medium or high intensity indicators.
Background noise
Presence, type and severity indicators.
Audio quality
Indicators describing recording quality.
Speaker overlap
Signals for overlapping speech.
Silence
Indicators for extended silent segments.
